iMBrace turns fragmented enterprise knowledge into trusted answers, AI agents, and intelligent workflows — self-hosted, on infrastructure you control. This organization is home to the open-source **Community Edition** and the components that make up the platform.

## Why open source

Enterprises should be able to build context-aware AI on their own knowledge, models, data, and tools — without handing any of it to a third party. Community Edition gives teams a transparent, self-hostable foundation: full data sovereignty and no closed ecosystem.

## The platform

- **DocIQ** — enterprise knowledge and document intelligence
- **InsightIQ** — context-aware AI agents and insights
- **FlowOps & CommsIQ** — workflow and communication automation
- **Model flexibility & MCP** — bring your own LLMs, tools, and connections
