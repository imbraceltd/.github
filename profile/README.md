Welcome to the **iMBrace** organization 👋

We build an **open-source Enterprise AI Operating System** that helps teams turn fragmented
knowledge into trusted answers, AI agents, and intelligent workflows — on infrastructure they
control.

## 🌍 Our vision

Make enterprise-grade AI more **accessible, composable, and accountable**. The platform brings
together four complementary ideas — **enterprise knowledge, AI-powered action, operational
insight, and responsible governance** — so teams can move from one-off prompting toward
repeatable, AI-enabled processes without committing to a closed ecosystem.

## 🤝 Get involved

We'd love to have you join our community:

- **Issues & Pull Requests** — every open repository is open for contributions. Read
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/imbraceltd/iMBrace/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) first,
  including the **license boundary** you must respect.
- **Code of Conduct** — the shared
  [standard](https://github.com/imbraceltd/iMBrace/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) we hold each other to.
- **Security** — found a vulnerability? Please email **security@imbrace.co**
  (see [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/imbraceltd/iMBrace/blob/main/SECURITY.md)) — do not open a public issue.
