<h2 align="center">Hi there, this is iMBrace 👋</h2>

<p align="center"><b>Open-Source Enterprise AI Operating System</b></p>

iMBrace Community Edition is a self-hosted, open-source AI Operating System for building context-aware AI agents and stateful DAG workflows on infrastructure you control. Connect local knowledge, integrate tools through native MCP, and work with private LLMs while keeping sensitive enterprise data within your environment.

- **[Community Edition](https://github.com/imbraceltd/iMBrace)** — the open-source hub, with quick start and the full component list
- **[Self-hosting guide](https://github.com/imbraceltd/iMBrace#quick-start--run-imbrace-with-docker-compose)** — bring up the whole stack with one Docker Compose file
- **[imbrace.co](https://www.imbrace.co)** — product and company
- **[Contributing](https://github.com/imbraceltd/iMBrace/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)** — how to get involved, and the license boundary to respect

### What's inside

- **DocIQ** — enterprise knowledge and document intelligence
- **InsightIQ** — context-aware AI agents and insights
- **FlowOps & CommsIQ** — workflow and communication automation
- **Model flexibility & MCP** — bring your own LLMs, tools, and connections

Build on your own knowledge, models, and tools — with full data sovereignty and no closed ecosystem. Star the [Community Edition](https://github.com/imbraceltd/iMBrace), open an issue to say hi, or pick something to work on. We're happy to help you get started.
